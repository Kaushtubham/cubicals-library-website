Questions students ask

What are the timings?

The library runs from 7 AM to 10 PM. Each plan covers a set slot within those hours, and the full-day plans cover all of it.

What is the difference between fixed and flexible seats?

A fixed seat is yours every day. A flexible seat lets you sit at any free seat when you arrive.

Can I keep my books here?

Yes. Lockers are available. Ask us about locker availability when you visit.

How do I find out the fees?

Fees are shared on enquiry. Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 94303 71915.

Can I visit before joining?

Yes. Message us to fix a time, and we will show you the library and available seats.
